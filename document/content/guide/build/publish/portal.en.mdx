The portal provides application access for signed-in users. Availability depends on the deployment's portal capability, license, plan, and resource permissions. Application publishing controls runtime content; portal visibility settings control the information users can see.

![Portal visibility controls](/imgs/optimized/current/portal-visibility.png.webp)

## Configure and verify

1. Open Application → Publishing Channels → Portal and copy the displayed access link.
2. Configure visibility for execution status, full response, citation excerpts, full citations, and source-document downloads according to the intended audience.
3. After publishing application configuration changes, open the link as a test member with application access and start a new conversation.
4. Check the answer and the execution and citation information visible to that member against your settings.

The portal home provides New Conversation and All Applications entrances. Available applications and models depend on the account and team configuration. For access errors, inspect login identity, team, application collaborator permissions, and portal licensing. For citations, also inspect knowledge-base and source access.

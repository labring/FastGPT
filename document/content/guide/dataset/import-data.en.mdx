This guide walks you through importing enterprise local documents (such as policies, product manuals, and training materials) into a FastGPT knowledge base, optimizing chunking parameters to ensure high retrieval recall.

## Supported Document Formats

FastGPT natively parses the following document types:
- **Plain Text and Lightweight Markup**: `.txt`, `.md`
- **Office Documents**: `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.pptx`
- **Web and Structured Exports**: `.html`, `.csv`

Keep individual file sizes under 50 MB for optimal processing. For large scanned files, run local OCR prior to upload to ensure clean text extraction.

## Step 1: Upload Local Files

1. Open the target knowledge base and click "New / Import" in the upper right corner.
2. Select "Text Dataset" as the data source, then click "Local Files".
3. Drag and drop or browse local files. The platform supports batch uploading multiple files simultaneously.
4. Wait until upload progress reaches 100%, then click "Next".

## Step 2: Choose Processing Mode

FastGPT offers two core processing paradigms suited to different conversational requirements:

### 1. Direct Chunking Mode
- **Mechanism**: Splits source text into individual chunks based on paragraphs and token limits, generating vector and full-text indexes directly.
- **Use Cases**: Policies, legal codes, academic papers, white papers, and long-form specifications.
- **Recommended Parameters**:
  - **Chunk Size**: Start with the current default and inspect complete clauses and table rows in the preview before adjusting.
  - **Chunk Boundaries**: Check cross-paragraph references and duplicated text; available controls depend on the selected split mode.
  - **Custom Delimiters**: Use `\n\n` or custom header markers to prioritize natural chapter breaks.

### 2. QA Extraction Mode
- **Mechanism**: Calls a language model in the background to read text segments and generate structured "Question-Answer" pairs, generating embeddings on the extracted questions.
- **Use Cases**: Customer support FAQ lists, operational cheat sheets, and product feature lookup tables.
- **Recommended Parameters**: Choose a reliable chat model and craft concise extraction instructions to ensure generated pairs remain faithful to source material.

## Step 3: Preview Data and Verify Chunk Quality

Click "Next" to open the data preview screen. Inspect chunk quality before writing records into the vector database:

1. **Paragraph Coherence**: Click file items on the left and check whether sentences end naturally within chunks on the right.
2. **Tables and Lists**: Confirm that Markdown tables, numbering sequences, and section headers remain aligned.
3. **Parameter Tuning**: If chunks appear overly fragmented or excessively long, click "Back" in the lower left to modify token limits and recompute the preview instantly.

## Step 4: Submit Training and Track Progress

1. Once the preview meets your quality standards, click "Start Import".
2. The platform queues asynchronous background tasks. Monitor progress in the collection list:
   - "Processing (X%)" indicates active text extraction and vector generation.
   - Status changing to "Ready" confirms that both vector and full-text indexes are persisted.
3. If a collection reports an "Error" status, open "View Error Details" in the collection menu to troubleshoot model balance or network timeouts, then click "Retry".

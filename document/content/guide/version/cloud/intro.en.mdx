## Cloud Service Capabilities and Edition Comparison

FastGPT Cloud provides a fully managed AI application platform with pre-configured language models, embedding models, rerankers, enhanced PDF parsing, and Agent virtual machine sandboxes out of the box.

| Dimension | FastGPT Cloud Service | Self-Hosted (Community / Commercial) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Infrastructure & Operations** | Fully managed by the FastGPT team with high availability, elastic scaling, and seamless version upgrades | Deployed on your own servers or private cloud; requires self-managed MongoDB, VectorDB, S3, sandbox, and upgrades |
| **Model Provisioning** | Pre-integrated LLMs, embedding models, rerankers, and enhanced PDF parsing billed via AI points | Configured by operators via [AIProxy / OneAPI](/en/self-host/config/model/intro) or local Ollama |
| **Feature Scope** | Includes enterprise orchestration, third-party dataset sync, evaluations, log dashboards, and team permissions (excluding SSO and custom branding; reranking and website datasets require a paid plan) | Community Edition provides core orchestration and datasets; enterprise features require a [Commercial License](/en/guide/version/commercial) |
| **Billing & Statements** | Flexible team subscription plans and add-on resource packs; see [Usage and Billing](/en/guide/workspace/usage-and-billing) | Free open-source Community Edition; Commercial Edition licensed by deployment tier and term |

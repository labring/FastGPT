## Cloud Service Capabilities and Edition Comparison

FastGPT Cloud provides a fully managed AI application platform with pre-configured language models, embedding models, rerankers, enhanced PDF parsing, and Agent virtual machine sandboxes out of the box.

| Dimension | Mainland China Cloud (`fastgpt.cn`) | International Cloud (`fastgpt.io`) | Self-Hosted (Community / Commercial) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Account & Data Residency** | Independent account system hosted in Mainland China | Independent account system hosted on international infrastructure | Hosted on your own servers or private cloud |
| **Model Provisioning** | Pre-integrated models billed via AI points | Pre-integrated global models billed via AI points | Configured by operators via [AIProxy / OneAPI](/en/self-host/config/model/intro) or local Ollama |
| **Operations & Upgrades** | Managed availability, scaling, and seamless upgrades | Managed availability, scaling, and seamless upgrades | Self-managed MongoDB, VectorDB, S3, sandbox, and upgrades |
| **Billing & Invoicing** | Subscription plans, add-on packs, and VAT invoicing; see [Usage and Billing](/en/guide/workspace/usage-and-billing) | International subscription plans and billing statements | Free Community Edition; enterprise capabilities via [Commercial License](/en/guide/version/commercial) |

The citation reader helps verify an answer, locate matched chunks, and inspect accessible sources. These screenshots use demonstration knowledge about service hours and refund rules.

# Why a Chunk Reader?

Click a source below an answer to inspect its evidence. Available content depends on the source, publishing settings, and access permissions.

## Limitations of Traditional Citations

An excerpt is useful for checking a specific fact. When context is needed, use the full-text reader with the required permission.

![Visitor access to cited excerpts](/imgs/current/citation-excerpt-only.png)

## FastGPT Chunk Reader: Precise Positioning, Seamless Reading

The reader highlights the current citation and provides a citation index and navigation controls. Check that the facts in the answer agree with the highlighted text.

![Answer and full-text citation reader](/imgs/current/citation-reader-full-text.png)

# Core Features



## Full-Text Display & Positioning

Click a source filename below the answer to open its highlighted excerpt. With full-text visibility enabled, you can also inspect the surrounding context.

![First citation and surrounding context](/imgs/current/citation-first-chunk.png)

## Citation Navigation

Use the previous and next controls at the upper right to move between matched chunks. The index shows positions such as `1/2` and `2/2`; the corresponding control is disabled at either boundary.

![Navigation to the second citation](/imgs/current/citation-second-chunk.png)

## Citation Quality Scoring

Inspect retrieval scores in the knowledge base **Search Test** or in the knowledge-search execution details of a conversation. Score meanings vary by retrieval channel. Hover a score label for its explanation; this screenshot shows a rerank tooltip in Search Test.

![Search results and score explanation](/imgs/current/dataset-result-scores.png)

## One-Click Document Export

Open the source menu at the upper right. With the required permissions, it offers **Download data**, **Open original**, or **Go to knowledge base**. Availability depends on the source and access settings.

![Citation source actions](/imgs/current/citation-source-menu.png)

# Advanced Features



## Flexible Visibility Control

Configure citation visibility in the [public share link](/en/guide/build/publish/link). Excerpt visibility exposes cited fragments; full-text visibility enables document reading and citation navigation. Original-file access has its own visibility setting. Verify the result as a visitor.

![Excerpt-only visitor reader](/imgs/current/citation-excerpt-only.png)

## Instant Annotation

Users with knowledge-base editing permission can choose **Go to knowledge base** and edit the content and indexes. After saving, wait for processing to finish, run a new search, and verify that the result uses the updated content.

![Knowledge content and index editor](/imgs/current/citation-edit-data.png)

## Smart Document Performance

Long documents load content as needed for the reading position. After navigating between citations, wait for loading to finish before checking the highlight and context. Network conditions, document length, and service response affect loading time.

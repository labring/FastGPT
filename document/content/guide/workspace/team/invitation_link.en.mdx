---
title: Member Invitation Links
description: Create, manage, and revoke team invitation links to onboard collaborators into a FastGPT workspace.
---

Team Owners and members granted the Team **Admin** permission (verified in the **Admin** column under **Account → Team management → Permissions**; see [Members, Departments, Groups, and Permissions](/en/guide/workspace/team/team_roles_permissions)) can invite collaborators by generating **member invitation links**. Teams running in Sync mode or WeCom mode are managed by external directory synchronization and do not display the **Invite Members** button.

## How to Use

1. **Navigate to "Account → Team management → Members" in the left navigation bar and click the "Invite Members" button in the upper-right corner.**

![How to Use — interface example](/imgs/optimized/current/team-invitation-empty.png.webp)

2. **In the invitation dialog, click "Create Invitation Link" to generate a new link**

![How to Use — interface example](/imgs/optimized/current/team-invitation-create.png.webp)

3. **Fill in the details**

![How to Use — interface example](/imgs/optimized/current/team-invitation-expiration.png.webp)

Link description: We recommend describing the intended use case or purpose. The description cannot be changed after creation.

Expiration: 30 minutes, 7 days, or 1 year

Usage limit: 1 person or unlimited

4. **Click "Copy Link" and send it to the people you want to invite**

![How to Use — interface example](/imgs/optimized/current/team-invitation-result.png.webp)

5. **When a user visits the link, they will be redirected to the login page if not logged in or registered. After logging in, they will be taken to the team page to handle the invitation.**

> Invitation links look like: fastgpt.cn/account/team?invitelinkid=xxxx

![Accept or ignore the team invitation](/imgs/optimized/guide/team_permissions/invitation_link/image5.png.webp)

Enter a "Team member name" (20 characters max) in the dialog and click "Join team" to join the team.

Click "Reject invitation" to close the dialog. The user can still accept the invitation by visiting the link again later.

## Link Expiration and Auto-Cleanup

### Why Links Expire

- Manually disabled by a Team Admin.
- Automatically disabled upon reaching its expiration time.
- A single-use link (1-person limit) has already been used by a member to join the team.

Disabled links cannot be accessed or re-enabled.

### Link Limits

Each user can have up to 10 **active** invitation links at a time.

### Auto-Cleanup

Disabled links are automatically deleted after 30 days.

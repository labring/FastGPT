FastGPT Cloud and self-hosted Commercial Edition deployments with `customDomain` enabled support custom domain configuration.

## How to Configure a Custom Domain

### 1. Open the "Custom Domain" Page

In the left sidebar, go to **Account → Custom Domain** (visible in FastGPT Cloud and Commercial Edition deployments with `customDomain` enabled) to open the configuration page.

If your plan does not support this feature, follow the on-screen instructions to upgrade.

![Open configuration page](/imgs/optimized/current/custom-domain-list.png.webp)

### 2. Add a Custom Domain

1. Have your domain ready. Your domain must have an ICP filing. Currently supported filing providers are Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, and Volcano Engine.
2. Click **Add** to create a domain binding. For a domain that is not yet active, open **Edit** for that row.
3. In the dialog, select your ICP filing provider under **Domain Filing Provider** (Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, or Volcano Engine).
4. Enter your domain, such as `www.example.com`.
5. In your domain provider's DNS management console, add the `CNAME` record shown on the screen.
6. After adding the DNS record, click **Save**. The system automatically verifies the DNS configuration within about a minute; if verification times out, click retry.
7. Once the status next to the domain input shows **Verified**, click **Confirm** to finish.

![Configure custom domain](/imgs/optimized/current/custom-domain-form.png.webp)

You can now access FastGPT services and call FastGPT APIs using your own domain.

## DNS Resolution Failure

The system checks DNS resolution daily. If the DNS record becomes invalid, the custom domain status changes to **Inactive**. Click **Edit** on the **Custom Domain** management page to re-verify the DNS record.

![Edit](/imgs/optimized/guide/team_permissions/customDomain/3.png.webp)

To change your custom domain or switch providers, delete the existing configuration and set it up again.

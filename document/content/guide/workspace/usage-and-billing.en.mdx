In the **Account** center, team owners and members can monitor AI point consumption, manage subscriptions and add-on resource packs, request invoices, and maintain personal profile and preference settings.

## 1. Usage Details and Dashboard

Open **Account → Usage Records** to switch between **Usage Details** and the **Usage Dashboard**:

### 1. Usage Details

- **Multi-dimensional filters**: Filter by team member, usage source (editor debug, portal chat, share link, OpenAPI, knowledge indexing, automated evaluation, MCP call), project name, and date range.
- **Line-item breakdown**: Inspect timestamp, member, source, target application or knowledge base, and consumed AI points, and expand any row to view input/output token counts and unit rates.
- **CSV export**: Export filtered usage records to CSV for internal cost allocation and financial reconciliation.

### 2. Usage Dashboard

- Visualize daily or monthly AI point consumption trends, top applications by usage, model call distribution, and knowledge indexing share to spot high-cost workflows or unexpected traffic spikes.

## 2. Subscription Plans and Add-On Packs

Open **Plans & Pricing** to inspect your team's active plan tier and resource quotas:

| Resource Category | Included Capabilities | Scaling and Activation Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard Plans (Monthly / Yearly)** | Team member cap, application limit, knowledge base dataset capacity, included AI points, and sandbox concurrency quotas | Plan upgrades take effect immediately; exact tier quotas follow your environment's pricing page |
| **Extra AI Point Packs** | Standalone top-up packs of AI compute points | Consumed automatically when plan-included points are exhausted, with selectable quantity and validity period |
| **Extra Knowledge Index Packs** | Additional vector index capacity for knowledge bases | Stacks onto your team's total dataset index quota during the pack's validity window |

The **AI Point Pricing Table** at the bottom of the pricing page lists per-1K-token point rates across language models, embedding models, rerankers, speech models, and enhanced PDF parsing.

## 3. Bills and Invoice Management

Open **Account → Bills & Invoices** to manage billing records across three tabs:

1. **Bills**: View subscription orders and add-on purchases, payment status, paid amount, and validity dates.
2. **Invoices**: Select one or more paid bills to submit an invoice request and download issued PDF invoices.
3. **Invoice Headers**: Save standard or VAT special invoice headers (company name, tax ID, bank account, registered address, phone, and recipient email) for reuse.

## 4. Personal Profile, Security, and Preferences

| Section | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| **Account → Profile** | Update avatar, member display name, phone/email, and password; submit enterprise verification; redeem promo codes and inspect coupons |
| **Account → API Keys** | Create, edit, set point/expiration limits, and revoke member OpenAPI keys; see [Access App via API](/en/guide/build/publish/openapi) |
| **Account → Notifications** | Read team invitations, quota alerts, billing reminders, and system announcements |
| **Account → Preferences** | Switch UI language (Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese) and default timezone |
| **Account → Third-Party Connections** | Manage linked third-party login or external service authorizations |
| **Account → Account Cancellation** | Review owned teams and assets before completing account deletion |
